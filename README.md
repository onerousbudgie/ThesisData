# ThesisData

Data, analysis notebooks, scripts and drafts from my MASc thesis in water
engineering at the University of Guelph (Anita Taylor): metal contamination of
groundwater / well water in British Columbia under climate change - uranium,
saltwater intrusion, drought, flood and heat effects on metals in wells.

## Folders

- `BCData/` - the datasets and the analysis: Jupyter notebooks (exploratory
  data analysis, factor analysis, regression, UMAP, data-cleaning
  "file fixer" notebooks), Python scripts, cleaned and processed CSV/Excel
  data, correlation and summary tables, Power BI files, figures, and climate
  station data downloaded from the Pacific Climate Impacts Consortium data
  portal (`pcds_*`) and BC government sources (`BCGW_*`, aquifer data).
  See `BCData/licence.txt` and `BCData/README.txt` for the source data terms.
- `PapersToPublish/` - drafts and supporting tables/figures for the papers
  from the thesis.
- `AdditionalNotes/` - my own notes for the literature review (the published
  papers by other authors that sit in this folder are not included here).
- Top level - thesis drafts and versions, proposal, methodology, outline,
  appendix, exploratory data analysis write-ups and paper drafts.
